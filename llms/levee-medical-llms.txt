# Levee Medical

> Levee Medical, Inc. is a privately held, clinical-stage medical device company in
> Durham, North Carolina, developing the Voro Urologic Scaffold — a bioabsorbable
> implant designed to support and stabilize the bladder neck and urethra after radical
> prostatectomy, to accelerate the return of continence and reduce chronic
> post-prostatectomy urinary incontinence.

## What this company is

- Founded 2018; headquartered at 4819 Emperor Blvd, Suite 400, Durham, NC 27703.
- Sole product: the Voro Urologic Scaffold, an absorbable device placed during
  robotic-assisted radical prostatectomy. It is INVESTIGATIONAL — not FDA approved and
  not available for commercial sale.
- Under evaluation in ARID II, an FDA-approved IDE multicenter randomized controlled
  pivotal study.
